### 3. Prompt Mapping Enhancement Guide Created
**File:** `docs/padna_refine/v2.1_prompt_mapping_guide.md`

**Key Enhancements Specified:**

**Freckles (High-Priority):**
- Enhanced descriptors: "heavy freckles, prominent across face" vs. "heavy freckle density"
- Composite strategy: Combine Density + Coverage + Contrast into single emphatic phrase
- Example output: "prominent freckles with medium density across face, neck, and shoulders"

**Iris Detail (High-Priority):**
- Enhanced base color descriptors: "striking light green eyes" vs. "light green iris"
- Composite strategy: BaseColor + Flecks + Pattern + Ring → single complex descriptor
- Example output: "striking light green eyes with hazel flecks scattered throughout iris, moderate limbal ring adding depth"

**Skin Texture (High-Priority):**
- New mapping: "natural skin texture, visible pores, realistic skin detail"
- Anti-airbrushing negative prompts: "NOT airbrushed, NOT overly smooth, NOT plastic-looking skin"

**Makeup Eyes (Medium-High):**
- Enhanced smoky-eye: "heavy dramatic smoky eye with thick black eyeliner, intense eye makeup"
- Lash descriptors: "long volumized lashes with dramatic mascara or false lashes"

**Makeup Lips (Medium-High - NEW v2.1):**
- Finish container added: matte/gloss/shimmer
- Composite strategy: Color + Finish → "nude pink glossy lips with shiny finish"

**Headpiece (NEW v2.1):**
- Formalized: "wearing elegant tiara", "wearing ornate crown", etc.

**Apparel Themes (NEW v2.1):**
