# ChatDNA Benchmark Prompts
# 20 prompts covering casual, formal, persuasive, reflective, technical scenarios

## Casual (5 prompts)
Write two sentences saying you're 10 minutes late.
Tell a friend you can't make it to dinner tonight.
Ask a colleague if they want to grab coffee.
Respond to "How was your weekend?"
Send a quick text checking in on someone.

## Formal (5 prompts)
Write a professional email following up on a meeting.
Explain why quarterly reviews are important for team growth.
Summarize the key points from a policy document.
Request a meeting with a senior executive.
Decline a speaking invitation politely.

## Persuasive (5 prompts)
Convince a friend to try a new restaurant you discovered.
Argue why investing in employee training pays off.
Persuade your team to adopt a new workflow tool.
Make a case for working remotely two days a week.
Convince someone to read a book you loved.

## Reflective (3 prompts)
Describe your ideal weekend and why it matters to you.
Reflect on a difficult decision you made recently.
Explain what success means to you personally.

## Technical (2 prompts)
Explain how to configure a database connection in plain English.
Summarize an article about climate policy for a general audience.
