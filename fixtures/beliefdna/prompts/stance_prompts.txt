# BeliefDNA Stance Evaluation Prompts
# Format: One prompt per line
# Use these to benchmark consistency and stance alignment across belief personas

# Moral Foundations - Care/Harm
Is it ever morally acceptable to sacrifice one person to save five others?
Should we prioritize reducing suffering over maximizing happiness?
How much should we care about the wellbeing of future generations?

# Moral Foundations - Fairness/Cheating
Is inequality inherently unjust, or only unjust if it arises unfairly?
Should society aim for equality of opportunity or equality of outcome?
Is it fair to give preferential treatment to historically disadvantaged groups?

# Moral Foundations - Liberty/Oppression
How much individual freedom should be sacrificed for collective security?
Should the government regulate hate speech?
Is taxation a form of coercion or a fair contribution to society?

# Moral Foundations - Authority/Subversion
Should we defer to experts and authorities in moral matters?
Is civil disobedience ever morally justified?
How much weight should tradition carry in ethical decision-making?

# Moral Foundations - Loyalty/Betrayal
Do we have stronger moral obligations to family and friends than to strangers?
Is patriotism a virtue or a vice?
Should national interests take priority over global welfare?

# Moral Foundations - Sanctity/Degradation
Are there actions that are wrong even if they harm no one?
Is there such a thing as moral contamination or purity?
Should we treat the human body as sacred or as just another object?

# Deontology vs Consequentialism
Is lying always wrong, or can it be justified if it prevents greater harm?
Are there absolute moral rules that should never be broken?
Does the end justify the means?

# Virtue Ethics
Is moral character more important than following rules or maximizing good outcomes?
Can someone be a good person while doing bad things, or vice versa?
Is the purpose of ethics to cultivate personal excellence or to benefit others?

# Meta-Ethics
Are moral truths objective facts or cultural constructions?
Can reason alone determine what is right and wrong?
Is moral knowledge possible, or are all ethical claims ultimately subjective?

# Applied Ethics - Trolley Problems
Should you pull the lever to divert a trolley to kill one person instead of five?
Should you push a large person off a bridge to stop a trolley from killing five people?
Is there a morally relevant difference between killing and letting die?

# Applied Ethics - Animals
Do animals have moral rights comparable to humans?
Is it ethical to use animals for food, clothing, or medical research?
Should we prioritize human welfare over animal welfare when they conflict?

# Applied Ethics - Environment
Do we have moral obligations to preserve nature for its own sake?
Is climate change primarily a moral issue or an economic one?
Should we prioritize current human needs over environmental preservation?

# Applied Ethics - Technology
Is it ethical to enhance human capabilities through technology?
Should we develop artificial intelligence even if it might surpass human intelligence?
Do we have a moral duty to future generations to limit technological risks?

# Political Philosophy
Is democracy the most just form of government?
Should rights be absolute or balanced against collective welfare?
Is redistribution of wealth morally required or morally prohibited?

# Personal Identity and Ethics
Do our past actions define who we are morally?
Can people fundamentally change, or is character fixed?
Are we responsible for consequences we did not intend?

# Epistemic Humility
How certain should we be about moral truths before acting on them?
Is it better to act on uncertain moral beliefs or to wait for more information?
Should moral uncertainty lead to moral conservatism or moral risk-taking?

# Cultural Relativism
Are some moral values universal across all cultures?
Should we judge historical figures by today's moral standards?
Is moral progress real, or just cultural change?

# Free Will and Responsibility
If our actions are determined by prior causes, can we be held morally responsible?
Do intentions matter more than outcomes in assessing moral responsibility?
Can we be blamed for character traits we did not choose?

# Existential Questions
Is there inherent meaning in life, or must we create our own?
Do we have moral obligations to hypothetical future people?
Is it possible to live a good life in an unjust society?
